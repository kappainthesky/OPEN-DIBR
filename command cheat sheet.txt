===========================================================
FAN DATASET
===========================================================

[1] Normal Rendering

.\RealtimeDIBR.exe -i ../../examples/Fan -j ../../examples/Fan/example_opengl.json --triangle_deletion_margin 10


-----------------------------------------------------------

[2] Show Input Cameras

.\RealtimeDIBR.exe -i ../../examples/Fan -j ../../examples/Fan/example_opengl.json --show_inputs --triangle_deletion_margin 10


-----------------------------------------------------------

[3] Static Rendering (Frame 50)

.\RealtimeDIBR.exe -i ../../examples/Fan -j ../../examples/Fan/example_opengl.json --static --frame_nr 50 --triangle_deletion_margin 10


-----------------------------------------------------------

[4] Input Culling (Use Only 2 Cameras)

.\RealtimeDIBR.exe -i ../../examples/Fan -j ../../examples/Fan/example_opengl.json --max_nr_inputs 2 --triangle_deletion_margin 10


-----------------------------------------------------------

[5] FPS Measurement

.\RealtimeDIBR.exe -i ../../examples/Fan -j ../../examples/Fan/example_opengl.json --fps_csv ./fan_fps.csv --triangle_deletion_margin 10


-----------------------------------------------------------

[6] Save Output View

.\RealtimeDIBR.exe -i ../../examples/Fan -j ../../examples/Fan/example_opengl.json -p ../../examples/Fan/example_output.json -o .

===========================================================
PARAMETER EXPERIMENTS
===========================================================

Triangle Deletion Margin

5     -> --triangle_deletion_margin 5
10    -> --triangle_deletion_margin 10
20    -> --triangle_deletion_margin 20


-----------------------------------------------------------

Mesh Resolution

Highest Quality

--mesh_subdivisions 0

Medium

--mesh_subdivisions 1

Fast

--mesh_subdivisions 2


-----------------------------------------------------------

Input Cameras

All Cameras

--max_nr_inputs -1

Use 4 Cameras

--max_nr_inputs 4

Use 2 Cameras

--max_nr_inputs 2

Use 1 Camera

--max_nr_inputs 1


===========================================================
KEYBOARD CONTROLS
===========================================================

W A S D      Move Camera
Q / Z        Forward / Backward
Left Mouse   Rotate Camera
Mouse Wheel  Zoom
Esc          Exit

G            Decrease Triangle Deletion Margin
H            Increase Triangle Deletion Margin

B            Decrease Blending Factor
N            Increase Blending Factor

C            Slow Camera
V            Speed Up Camera

R            Toggle Show Input Camera Window
(only when --show_inputs is enabled)
